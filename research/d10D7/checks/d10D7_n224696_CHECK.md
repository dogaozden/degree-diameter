# (10,7) ≥ 224,696: the table construction over H(8) plus one pair of hub vertices

Construction X(M) + hubs of the exp2-squad (research/squads/exp2/hexd7/README.md on master; tool d7hex.c copied here
unchanged): vertices (v, i), v a point or line of the split Cayley hexagon H(8), i ∈ Z3; (p,i) ~ (l,i) for p on l,
(p,i) ~ (M(p), i+1) for a bijection M (points -> lines). One freed point p: its three M-edges are deleted and two
hubs added, z ~ (p,0),(p,1),(p,2) and z' ~ (M(p),0),(M(p),1),(M(p),2), with the algebraic cover around the freed
pair (S'(p) -> S(M(p))). 6·37,449 + 2 = 224,696 vertices, max degree 10.

H(8) needs characteristic 2, which research/p4/d6/hexgen.c (q = p or p², p odd) does not build; hexgen2.c here does
(Moore sphere sizes checked). That is why (10,7) was not in the exp2 runs.

Files: research/frontier/sols/d10D7_hex8_K1_n224696.sol (d7hex format: header, freed point, M), and
research/d10D7/graphs/d10D7_n224696_hex8_K1.edges.gz (edge list, first line n).

Rebuild and check (from research/d10D7):
    gcc -O2 -o hexgen2 hexgen2.c && mkdir -p hex && ./hexgen2 8          # H(8) -> hex/hex_q8.bin
    gcc -O3 -o d7hex d7hex.c -lm && ./d7hex edges hex/hex_q8.bin ../frontier/sols/d10D7_hex8_K1_n224696.sol x.edges
    gcc -O3 -o apsp ../lifts/coordinator_check/apsp.c && ./apsp x.edges 10 7
