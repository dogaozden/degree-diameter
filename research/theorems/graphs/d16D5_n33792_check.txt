Bipartite graph, maximum degree 16, diameter 5, 33,792 vertices (beat-16-5-squad, 2026-10-24).
Beats 31,460 (PG(4,3) flag graph, arXiv:2608.03965 / 2608.27253) and the 32,768 double cover of the LANL (16,4) graph.

Construction (build_d16D5_n33792.py, self-contained, no repo imports):
  base K_{3,3} + perfect matching (3+3 vertices, 12 edges); controller = voltage lift by Z_22 (66+66 vertices,
  4-regular, letters from the base edge); route-chart lift with fibre F_4^4 and the chart in the script
  (8 singular nonbacktracking patterns, pencil-complete).  Lift vertex ((a_i,g),x) ~ ((b_j,g+v_e), A_c x + lam b_c).
