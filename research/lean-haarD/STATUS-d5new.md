## (7,5) on 1200 vertices: Haar graph of SL(2,5) × Z_5
- `S` = `research/beyond-haar-d5/records/d7D5_n1200_haar_sl5xc5.txt` (header `7 5 sl 5 n 5 N 600`).
  `research/frontier/build_haar2.py` on it: ecc 5 from both sides, unreached 0, n = 1200, degree 7, no repeated edge,
  `VALID d=7 D=5`, edge-list sha256 `a56affd8…adf0` (same as the job's own run).
- Convention check: build_haar2.py joins `(g,A)` to `(s g, B)`, matrices `[[a,b],[c,d]]` multiplied as matrices, `z` added;
  this is `Haar.haarGraph` (`inl g ~ inr h` iff `h g⁻¹ ∈ S`) with `SL5.Grp × Multiplicative (ZMod 5)`.
- Files, all under `lean/BipartiteDD/Haar/D7D5SL5/`, generated by `research/lean-haarD/gen_d7D5_1200.py`:
  `SL5Z5.lean` (numbering `5 * enc M + z`, `equiv600`; reuses `D6D7c.SL5`), `Cert.lean` (S, two 600-entry quadruple certificates
  for `a⁻¹ b c⁻¹ d` and `a b⁻¹ c d⁻¹`, `decide +kernel`, `cover1`, `cover2`), `Record1200.lean` (`equiv1200`, `nb_7_5_n1200`
  via `haar_isBipartiteDegDiam_of_equiv`), `Axioms.lean`, `Axioms.out.txt`.
- Build: `lake build BipartiteDD.Haar.D7D5SL5.Record1200`, about 1 minute.

## (5,5) on 360 vertices: lift by C15 × S3
- Not a Haar graph, so no Haar lemma. 360 vertices is small enough for the existing checker: the graph is the one produced
  by `research/beyond-haar-d5/records/build_d5D5_n360_z15s3.py` (its own BFS: 5-regular, bipartite, diameter 5), and it
  is checked in Lean by `Fast.check_of_fast` + `check_sound`, as G354 and G1140 are.
- `lean/gen_d5new.py` turns that script's edge list into `lean/BipartiteDD/Graphs/G360.lean` (same layout as `G354.lean`;
  imports `gen.py`, which is unchanged). I confirmed the Lean adjacency lists equal the edge list (900 edges), and
  `research/beyond-haar-d5/records/check_independent.py` on that edge list gives Δ = 5, D = 5.
- `lean/BipartiteDD/D5New/Record360.lean` has `nb_5_5_n360`; `Axioms.lean`, `Axioms.out.txt` next to it.
- Build: `lake build BipartiteDD.D5New.Record360`, about 20 seconds.
