## Obstacles and fixes (in the order they were hit)

1. **Group-level stage check runs out of memory.** The D7D8b style check, `decide (gOf n = wordC c)` on the group
   `SL(2,5) × PSL(2,7) × Z_13`, costs about 0.04 s per code and was killed (exit 137) at 5460 codes per stage. Switched to
   the D6D10c style: a check on `Nat` quadruples, with bridging lemmas. A stage then takes about 135 s, serially.
2. **PSL is a quotient, so the bridging relation is up to sign.** `EqP g x` says `g` has the entries of `x` or of `-x`.
   The bridging lemmas use `PSL7.mul_pm` and `PSL7.inv_pm`; the sign test is discharged by `PSL7.posB_neg` (`neg_quad_false`).
3. **Instance paths of `Prod`.** With `G` the `Prod` group, the simp lemmas `Prod.fst_mul` etc. produce `*` through the
   `Prod.instGroup` chain, and comparing that with the direct `PSL7.instMul` timed out at any heartbeat limit (the `canon`
   `dite` on symbolic entries is unfolded). Fixed by making `G` a structure with componentwise `Mul`, `One`, `Inv` and a
   `Group` instance built from them, so every projection uses the component's own operations.
4. **Projecting `prodS c` blows up.** `mulS` repeats its arguments, so `(prodS c).1` unfolds the whole nested product.
   Fixed by `generalize prodS c = Q at …` before projecting.
5. **Cover statement vs. lemma context.** `cover : CoverHyp SS` (defined in `Words`, which imports only the bare algebra
   modules) timed out at the lemma application, because its `Group` chain is elaborated in a different instance context
   from `Diam10`'s. Fixed by defining `CoverD` in `Covering.lean`, which imports `Diam10`, and bridging `wordC` to it with
   `wordOf_eq : wordOf … = x₁ * (x₂⁻¹ * …)`, which holds for every group by `rfl`.
