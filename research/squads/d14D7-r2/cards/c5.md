# c5: symmetric packings from automorphisms of H(11)
Goal as c1. Find automorphisms of H(11) as 7x7 matrices on Tits coordinates (COORDS from `../gh_build 11 COORDS`):
start with diagonal and monomial matrices preserving x0x4+x1x5+x2x6-x3^2 and test whether they map every hexagon line
(../data/gh_11.txt) to a line. Products give a group; compute its orbits on flags and search for packings that are
unions of orbits (orbit-level greedy/annealing: an orbit is allowed if all its flags' zones are pairwise disjoint and
disjoint from the chosen ones). Report the group found, the best VALID N and the flags file.
