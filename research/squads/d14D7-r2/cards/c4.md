# c4: algebraic partial ovoids for the q=11 packing
Goal as c1. Points of H(q) pairwise opposite never conflict on the point side. In Tits coordinates (run
`../gh_build 11 COORDS > /dev/null` in your folder: COORDS gets one line per point index with its normalized vector
x0..x6 mod 11, quadric x0x4+x1x5+x2x6 = x3^2), points with x0 = 1 are u = (x1,x2,x3,x5,x6) in GF(11)^5, and two of them
are at distance <= 4 iff Q'(u-v) = 0 with Q'(w) = w3^2 - w1w5 - w2w6 (check this numerically on a few pairs against BFS
distances first). Find large sets with no isotropic differences (structured: cosets of anisotropic planes, orbits of a
linear map preserving Q', graphs of functions, unions of such), then choose one flag line per point so that the line
zones are disjoint (each pair of opposite points forbids one pair of lines: a small CSP; also allowed: chained pairs).
Report the best VALID N (flags file) and the structure.
