## 4. Assessment against the card
- (a) diameter 8 from generalized hexagons: NOT FOUND. The generalized-hexagon constructions in the record are diameter 6
  (Comellas-Gomez 1995; Gomez-Pelayo-Balbuena 1999; Pineda-Villavicencio et al. 2009; Pelayo 2000 H_q(K_h) of diameter 6/7) and diameter 5
  (Delorme EJC 1985, via Isham et al. 2026).
- (b) operation "diameter +1, degree kept, order x about (1 + degree/2)": NOT FOUND. The compound families in 3d-3e multiply the
  diameter. The factor (1 + Delta/2) is |V|+|E| arithmetic: |V(X)| + |E(X)| = |V(X)|(1 + Delta/2) for a Delta-regular X; no source
  found that describes a vertex+edge operation with diameter +1 for bipartite graphs. Line-digraph and Kautz methods in the record
  are for directed graphs (Pelayo 2000 sec. 1.5.2; Fiol, Yebra, Alegre); Gomez's bipartite GC families are Kautz/De Bruijn-inspired
  but have diameter factor k.
- Size remark (my arithmetic): for Delta = q+3, 5*floor((q+5)/2)*|GH(q)| ~ 5*Delta^6, which is about (5/(2 Delta)) times the bipartite
  Moore bound 2*Delta^7 for D = 8. Also |GH(q)|*(1+Delta/2) ~ Delta^6 is about half of the bipartite Moore bound for D = 7 (~2 Delta^6),
  consistent with the card's reading that the DGQ value is V+E of a diameter-7 base.
