# c03 — hubs on the (16,5) graph, designed from the chart algebra
Hub rules as in research/squads/beyond-small/r1/c01.md. Your twist: design hub neighbourhoods algebraically. A B-vertex
(v, y) reaches A-controller vertices w by controller walks of length 1 and 3; a nonsingular length-3 pattern reaches a
3-dimensional affine subspace (64 of 256 points) of w's fibre. If a hub is joined to (v, y + λc) for λ ∈ F4 (4 points
of one fibre along a direction c), the four 3-spaces are translates; when c is transverse to a pattern's 3-space they
tile w's whole fibre. Find directions/vertex choices so that 4 groups of 4 (+1 spare) cover every w ∈ A completely.
Compute coverage exactly (BFS or algebra), turn it into as many disjoint hubs as possible, check hub–hub conditions,
and verify with apsp. Report the largest valid n.
Time box: 40 min. Folder: research/squads/beyond-small/r1/c03/.
