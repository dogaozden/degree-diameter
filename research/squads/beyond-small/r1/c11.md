# c11 — Haar graphs at (17,5) with orbit-closed S (haar_sym)
research/structured/haar_sym.c (README.md, STRUCTURE.md in that folder) searches H(G,S) with S = {e} ∪ fixed elements ∪
orbits of an automorphism α. It broke the random wall at D = 7..10. Try it at D = 5, Δ = 17, groups of order just above
16,896: SL(2,q) × Z_n, PSL(2,q) × Z_n, the Heisenberg family, products (e.g. |SL(2,25)| = 15,600, |SL(2,27)| = 19,656,
|PSL(2,32)| = 32,736 too big; |PSL(2,31)| = 14,880 × ... ; pick orders 16,900..19,700). ORBIT types: k = 2, 4, 8, 16
(and 3) with f fixed. Short runs (each < 100 s, resume), log the best cost per (group, ORBIT). Check any 0 with
research/frontier/build_haar2.py and apsp.
Time box: 40 min. Folder: research/squads/beyond-small/r1/c11/.
