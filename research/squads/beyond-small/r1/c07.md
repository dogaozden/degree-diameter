# c07 — design charts for the 17 = 5+4+4+4 route chart
Read research/squads/beyond-small/r1/c04.md. Your job is the CHART, not the controller. A chart = A_0..A_3 ∈ GL(4,F4),
b_0..b_3, plus the extra vector e for letter 0. For a nonbacktracking letter pattern (t1,t2,t3,t4) the lifted walks
reach a sumset; in the 16-regular chart it is all 256 points iff the pattern is nonsingular (chart_u8 has 8 singular
patterns of 216). With e added, compute for every pattern the exact number of fibre points reached (the sumset size,
on both sides, A-side and B-side conventions as in BEAT_16_5_SQUAD.md §2) and for diagonals (closed walks) and
distance-2 pairs (pencil lemma). Search charts + e that make the most patterns full (ideally all 216 + pencil-complete
+ every closed 4-pattern full). Output the best charts as files in the chart_u8.txt format plus a line with e, and a
one-line summary of how many patterns are full. Score: if you also build a lift (K3,3 + doubled matching over Z23 with
the voltages of build_d16D5_n33792.py as a start) and apsp says VALID, report n; otherwise score 0 and best_cost =
number of non-full patterns.
