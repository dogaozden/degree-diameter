# c12 — quotients of generalized hexagons (wildcard with a calibration)
H(q) (generalized hexagon, incidence graph (q+1)-regular, diameter 6, 2(q^6−1)/(q−1) vertices). If a group Γ of
automorphisms acts freely on points and lines and moves every vertex far, the quotient H(q)/Γ is (q+1)-regular and may
have diameter 5. H(16)/Γ with |Γ| ≈ 50..66 would give 34k–45k at (17,5). First calibrate at small q where the records
are known: H(3) (Δ=4, 728 vertices; record (4,5) = 162 → |Γ| = 4 gives 182), H(4) (Δ=5, 2,730; record (5,5) = 384 →
|Γ| = 7 gives 390). Build H(q) in Q(6,q) coordinates (points X0X4+X1X5+X2X6 = X3², lines = lines of Q(6,q) with
Grassmann coordinates p12=p34, p54=p32, p20=p35, p65=p30, p01=p36, p46=p31; or reuse research/d10D7/hexgen2.c), find
automorphisms by testing candidate 7×7 matrices (torus diagonals, signed coordinate permutations, unipotent root
elements) edge by edge, look for free subgroups, form the quotient (orbits → vertices) and check diameter with apsp.
Report what quotient sizes reach diameter 5 at q = 3, 4 (any value above the records would itself be a result), then
at q = 16 if the calibration looks good.
Time box: 40 min. Folder: research/squads/beyond-small/r1/c12/.
