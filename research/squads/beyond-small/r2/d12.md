# d12 — hubs with fewer old neighbours plus hub–hub edges
Trade-off: a hub with 12 old neighbours (3 lines) + up to 5 edges to opposite-side hubs. Fewer old vertices per hub means
more hubs fit in the budget (1,408 per side at 12), and hub–hub edges give same-side hubs common neighbours. Find
whether 3 lines plus hub neighbours can satisfy the cover condition (a B-hub neighbour g contributes A-vertices within
distance 2 of N(g)), design such a structure and check with apsp. Time box 40 min. Folder r2/d12/.
