# d10 — connectors (second-level vertices)
Hubs have a spare slot. A connector is a new vertex on the other side joined only to hubs (≤ 17 of them); A-hubs sharing a
B-connector are at distance 2. The connector itself must be within 4 of its own side and 5 of the other (check: via its
hubs' neighbourhoods). Design hubs + connectors (connectors are vertices too: +1 each) that beat a plain hub packing:
e.g. groups of 17 hubs per connector with relaxed pairwise conditions inside a group. Build and apsp-check.
Time box 40 min. Folder r2/d10/.
