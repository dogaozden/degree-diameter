# d04 — 17-neighbour hubs (spend the 17th slot on compatibility)
A hub may have 17 old neighbours. Use the 17th to raise compatibility: after a 4-line cover, add one more unused vertex
that maximizes R(h)'s reach (or that hits the most current hubs' R sets). This costs vertex budget (993 hubs per side
max instead of 1,056) but may raise the compatibility density and the clique. Measure density and packing size vs
16-vertex hubs on the same seeds; report the best valid graph. Time box 40 min. Folder r2/d04/.
