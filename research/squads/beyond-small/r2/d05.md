# d05 — hub–hub edges to rescue incompatible pairs
Hubs with 16 old neighbours have one spare slot. An A-hub h1 joined (via its spare slot) to a B-hub g is within 4 of
every A-hub h2 with N(h2) adjacent to N(g) (path h1–g–x–b'–h2), and B-hubs likewise. Design a joint packing of A-hubs
and B-hubs plus a matching of spare slots between them that lets more hubs in than the pairwise condition alone allows.
Start from hubpack outputs on both sides (or c03's/director's), count rescued pairs, build and check with apsp.
Time box 40 min. Folder r2/d05/.
