# d09 — non-line hubs with larger distance-2 reach
Lines are good for covering (condition 1) but compatibility (2) depends on R(h) = distance-≤2 set of N(h). Search hub
sets N (16–17 vertices, not necessarily lines: e.g. 2 lines + 8 points, or points from many controller vertices) that
cover the side AND have |R(h)| as large as possible; measure the compatibility density of such candidates vs line hubs
(hubpack's 0.937) and pack them. Report the best valid graph. Time box 40 min. Folder r2/d09/.
