# d16 — exact search on a structured sub-family
Restrict hubs to a structured family small enough to search exactly: e.g. hubs whose 4 lines lie in 4 fixed B-controller
vertices that are the 4 neighbours of one A-controller vertex w (they are pairwise at controller distance 2), with fixed
directions; enumerate all covering hubs of that family, build their compatibility graph and solve max clique exactly
(branch and bound in C). Repeat over families (choice of w, directions) and combine families. Report how many hubs one
family gives and the best valid graph. Time box 40 min. Folder r2/d16/.
