# d15 — annealing over whole packings (side A)
Treat the A-hub packing as the state: a set of disjoint 4-line hubs, each covering A. Moves: replace one hub by a newly
generated hub (greedy 4-line cover through a random line), or swap one line of a hub for another line that keeps its cover.
Energy = −(number of hubs) + λ·(number of incompatible same-side pairs); accept by Metropolis; at the end drop hubs to
remove every incompatible pair. Start from r2/hubsA_191.txt. Report the largest valid graph. Time box 40 min. Folder r2/d15/.
