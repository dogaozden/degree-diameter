# e07 — more symmetry
more symmetry. Find automorphisms of G beyond the Z22 shift (e.g. permutations of the base vertices a0 a1 a2 / b0 b1 b2 combined with voltage and fibre maps, fibre maps x → Mx + c that commute with the chart, or the Z22 involution), verify each by mapping every edge, and pack hub orbits under a larger group (orbits of 44, 66, …) so that fewer, larger orbit choices fill the slots. Report the group found and the best valid graph.
