# e06 — 17-neighbour orbit hubs
17-neighbour orbit hubs. Orbit bases N with 17 keys (4 lines + 1 vertex chosen to meet the most hubs' distance-2 sets) raise compatibility (r2/d04: 0.937 → 0.957 for single hubs). Re-pack orbits with 17-key bases (cap 45 per side) and/or upgrade existing bases with a 17th key from the unused keys; report whether it lets more orbits in, and the best valid graph.
