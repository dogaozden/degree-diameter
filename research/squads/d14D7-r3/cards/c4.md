(an elliptic quadric Q^-(3,11), 122 points, pairwise non-orthogonal), (b) ovoids of parabolic sections Q(4,11) of E

122), (c) unions/combinations of such structures that stay independent. Use them as seeds for local search
(add free vertices, plateau swaps) and compare with random starts. Score: best VALID N.
