# r3 c3: cyclic symmetry from the orthogonal group O^-(6,11)
Partial ovoids that are unions of orbits of a cyclic group. E spans the 6-dim space V = {x : x4 = -x0} with the
quadratic form Q(x) = x0x4 + x1x5 + x2x6 - x3^2 restricted to V (elliptic). Find isometries of (V, Q): products of
random reflections r_v(x) = x - B(x,v)/Q(v) v (Q(v) != 0) are isometries. Compute element orders and keep elements of
large order dividing q^3+1 = 1332 (e.g. 1332, 666, 444, 333, 222, 111, 37, 12...). Compute the orbits of a chosen
cyclic subgroup on the 15,984 points (projective points: normalize vectors), keep orbits that are independent sets,
then search for large unions of mutually independent orbits (exact or greedy), and finally extend by local search.
Score: best VALID N (and report the group order and orbit sizes).
