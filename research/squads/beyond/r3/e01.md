# e01: (3,11) above 1,008 by EXHAUSTIVE cubic Haar graphs of non-metacyclic groups
A cubic Haar graph is H(G, {e, x, y}). Up to isomorphism you may fix e and take {x, y} up to Aut(G); for groups of
order ≤ ~1,300 even all ordered pairs (|G|² ≤ 1.7M) are cheap with a bitset BFS (research/squads/b48/tools/haar_tab.c
reads any group table; research/squads/b48/tools/groups.py builds tables: psl2/sl2/pgl2 p, agl1 p, products with cyc n).
Target orders 505–1,100 (graph 1,010–2,200), D = 11. Groups to cover exhaustively (all pairs, report the minimum cost
per group): PSL(2,p) × Z_n, SL(2,p) × Z_n, PGL(2,p) × Z_n, AGL(1,p) × Z_n, SL(2,3) × Z_n, S4 × Z_n, A5 × Z_n, and
the 2-generated groups you can build, at those orders. r1/c11 already did metacyclic 350–400 and some sweeps (see its
folder); do not repeat them. Check any cost-0 graph with apsp (d = 3, D = 11). Score: largest valid graph; also a table
"group, order, minimum cost" (a certificate that the group fails is useful too).
