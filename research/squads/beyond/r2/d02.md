# d02: (20,5) by 5 letters × F4 (fibre F4^4 = 256, 5-regular controller)
Route-chart lifts (research/theorems/squad165/, BEAT_16_5_SQUAD.md §2): degree = letters × field size. Here 5 letters
over F4: a 5-regular bipartite controller with m vertices per side, lift 2·m·256 vertices. Nonbacktracking 4-walks per
vertex: 5·4³ = 320, so m ≤ 320; beating 75,000 needs m ≥ 147 (46 % of the bound; the (16,5) record had 66/108 = 61 %).
Do: (1) a chart over F4 with 5 letters (A_t ∈ GL(4,4), b_t), counting singular nonbacktracking 4-patterns (port
chart_search.c / r1/c04/chart5.c to 5 letters); (2) a 5-regular controller: voltage lift of a 5-regular bipartite base
multigraph (e.g. K5,5, or K3,3 plus a doubled matching plus a matching) by Z_k, annealed like mlift_chart.c for
"every same-side pair at distance 4 has a nonsingular NB 4-walk" (+ 4-cycle/pencil conditions); (3) build the lift
and check with apsp (d = 20, D = 5). Score: largest valid lift; report the best m even if below 147.
