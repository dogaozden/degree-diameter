## Notes

- Product form only: m = L*k needs gcd(L,k) = 1 (CRT to a cyclic group), so m = 25 (= 5*5) at q = 5 is not reachable in this family; the nearest reachable values are 24, 20, 21.
- Screen: each structure tried k from the largest coprime value (L*k <= 64) downward, 2 seeds per k; the first exact pass is kept. "null" means no pass among those seeds. A seed whose base graph repeats a (point, line, voltage) triple is rejected (DUP in screen_q5_checks.log); level-0 extra edges (s = 0) hit this often, so those rows are weakly tested.
- q = 7 confirm (confirm_q7.tsv): top three q = 5 structures. L = 3, (1,1) indep: exact PASS at m = 6 (k = 2, seed 1; COST 0 over 19608 sources). L = 4, (1,2) same: exact PASS only at m = 4 (k = 1); m = 12 and m = 20 fail with COST 1 to 2 (near misses). L = 3, (0,1) indep: no passing k (2 seeds).
- Best q = 5 design: L = 4, (1,2) same, k = 3, m = 12, seed 0: exact COST 0 over 3906 sources (lscan all). Its liftdiag is all zero (it passes), so it shows nothing.
- Weakest class (liftdiag on the m = 20 near miss at q = 7, L = 4, (1,2) same, k = 5, seed 0): sampled r:100:3, uncovered per source at hexagon distance 3 is 0.66 (level 0); at distance 5 it is 0.86 (level 1), 0.04 (level 2), 0.76 (level 3).
