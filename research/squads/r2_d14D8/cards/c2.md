# c2: rainbow-bundle hexagon voltages, then the level family
For two opposite lines M, L (hexagon distance 6) there are exactly q+1 geodesics (one through each point of M); the same
for opposite points. Write code that enumerates the geodesic bundles (at q = 5 all opposite pairs; at q = 7 a large
random sample) and computes, for hexagon voltages h: flags → Z_k, the number of DISTINCT voltages in each bundle.
Local search on h to maximize the worst / average bundle diversity (cheap objective: no lift check needed), for
k = 5, 6, 7. Then put the best h into the level family (opus/mk_lev.py style: hex (0, h), φ doubled with (+1, a), (+2, b),
a, b random or also optimized) and score with lifteval all (q = 5) and r:200 + all (q = 7). Compare with random h.
Report the largest exact m per q and whether bundle diversity predicts lift quality.
