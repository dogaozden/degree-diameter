# c3: algebraic voltages from coordinates
Use the coordinates (coord, GF(q), q prime) to define voltages with geometric meaning, inside the level family
(hex (0, h(p,l)), φ doubled with (+1, a(p)), (+2, b(p)), Z_5 × Z_k) and also in plain Z_m designs. Ideas (try many):
h(p, l) = projective parameter of p on l (a Möbius coordinate: l = span(u, v), p = u + t v, t ∈ PG(1,q)) composed with a
map PG(1,q) → Z_k (Singer identification PG(1,q) ≅ Z_{q+1} then mod k, or squares/non-squares, or t ↦ t + 1/t);
a(p), b(p) = ratio of two linear forms ℓ1(p)/ℓ2(p) (a bijection on almost every line) mapped into Z_k; φ = a bijection
that maps a-colour classes to lines with matching colours (see mk_ratio.py). Score each variant exactly at q = 5
(lifteval all) and q = 7 (r:200 then all); use liftdiag to see which (distance, level) classes stay uncovered.
Report the best exact m per q and the variant.
