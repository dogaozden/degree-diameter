# c6: geometric matchings φ
In all round-1 designs φ was a random bijection. Try geometric ones and compare at q = 5 (exact) and q = 7 (r:200):
(i) φ(p) = a line through p (the extra edges double hexagon flags; build disjoint perfect matchings with pmatch, see
mk_digon.py); (ii) φ(p) = a line at hexagon distance 3 from p chosen by a rule (e.g. via a fixed reference point or
the coordinates); (iii) φ(p) = a line at distance 5 (opposite-ish); (iv) Σ-equivariant random φ (hex/sym_q{q}.npz);
(v) φ built from the coordinate symmetry x ↦ (x4,x5,x6,x3,x0,x1,x2) or similar maps that send points to lines.
Use each φ in the level family (doubled, +1 and +2, random sub-voltages Z_k) and in plain Z_m with random voltages; find
the largest exact m and use liftdiag to explain the differences. Report the best φ type and its m per q.
