   gives two routes to the targets of y (hop at u2, 2 + 1 + 3 steps, or hop at u4, 4 + 1 + 1 steps), not one. Both are
   lost only if u2 and u4 are both freed, i.e. if the freed set contains a collinear path p ~ u2 ~ u4 on two different
   lines. A union of segments of pairwise opposite lines contains no such path. The full spread (7,300 points) is the
   largest such set: each freed point blocks at least q + 1/(q+1) lines, and the spread meets every line.
   Test (research/squads/d12D7/tools/orbcat.c on the graph with all 7,300 spread points freed, 2 hubs per point and
