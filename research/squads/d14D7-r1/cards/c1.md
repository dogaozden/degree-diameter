# c1: building scan at diameter 7 (Weyl-distance graphs)
The (16,5) squad wrote research/theorems/squad165/weyl_graphs.py (branch origin/claude/beat-16-5-squad-jiiwg2;
extract with git show into your folder). It enumerates bipartite Weyl-distance graphs of finite thick buildings
(side A = residues of type I, side B = residues of type J, adjacency = Weyl distance in a union X of double cosets)
and computes degrees, orders and exact diameter from the Coxeter group alone. Its scan() takes maxdeg, minorder, maxD.
Task: run it for maximum degree <= 14 and diameter <= 7, reporting every graph with more than 1,771,560 vertices,
and the 10 largest diameter-7 graphs of any order, and the largest diameter-8 graphs above 1.77M (near misses).
Extend the building list if it misses relevant types (e.g. twisted types, q up to 13). Check the selftest first.
Score: largest order of a diameter-<=7, degree-<=14 graph found (unit "vertices").
Compute: pure Python, run it through heavy.sh with a 900 s timeout (one run at a time).
