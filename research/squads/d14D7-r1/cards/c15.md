# c15: lifts of GH(q) with a re-voltaged or rewired flag matching (degree stays q+1)
GH(13) is 14-regular with 804,468 vertices; a 3-fold lift with diameter 7 would have 2,413,404 > 1,771,560. Pure lifts
of GH fail (girth 12 forces diameter >= 11). Variant: remove a perfect matching M0 of flags (one line per point, one
point per line) and replace it by a point->line matching sigma that is NOT a set of flags, then lift by Z_m with
voltage h on sigma and 0 elsewhere (or other voltage patterns). Test the small analogues where Moore bounds make the
answer clear: GH(2) (Delta 3; (3,7) record 168, Moore 254) and GH(3) (Delta 4; (4,7) record 840, Moore 2,186) with
m = 2, 3. Note: glift supports GH + extra matchings; to remove M0 you need your own small checker (or write the lift
edge list in Python and run apsp). Explain why it fails or report what it reaches.
Score: lowest number of pairs at distance > 7 found for a GH(3)-based variant with m >= 2 (unit "bad pairs"), with
vertex count in the idea line.
Compute: at most 4 heavy runs of <= 300 s.
