- The construction is in J. Gómez's PhD thesis (UPC 1986), public at
  https://web.mat.upc.edu/francesc.comellas/delta-d/papers/Gomez1986-PhD-red.pdf, §2.9.3 (thesis pp. 43–46, PDF pp.
  49–52, Figures 2.21a/b). Thesis p. 46 lists ΩH₅ N(8,8) ≥ 195,300, ΩH₇ N(10,8) ≥ 1,176,480, ΩH₈ N(11,8) ≥ 2,246,940,
  ΩH₉ N(12,8) ≥ 4,650,100, ΩH₁₁ N(14,8) ≥ 14,172,480, ΩH₁₃ N(16,8) ≥ 36,201,060: exactly the table values.
- Round 1's guess (a lift of "H(9) + 2 hop matchings" by a group of order 35) was wrong: ΩB₁ is not a lift.

**ΩB₁** (B₁ bipartite, even diameter D₁, N₁ vertices, Δ₁ + 2 = 2α): 5α copies of B₁ in α groups of 5, plus 5 new
vertices (t,k), k ∈ Z₅, for every vertex t of B₁. In a group of type (a) the new vertex of a point t is joined to t
in copies k−1, k+1 and that of a line t to t in copies k+2, k+3; type (b) swaps the patterns (points k+3, k+4; lines
k−1, k+1). Every copy vertex gets 2 new edges, every new vertex degree 2α. N = 5(α+1)N₁, Δ = Δ₁ + 2, claimed
D ≤ D₁ + 2. For B₁ = H(9): 30 copies of the 132,860-vertex incidence graph + 664,300 new vertices = 4,650,100.

**Why the diameter is 9.** A new vertex (t,k) is adjacent only to copies of t, in 2α of the 5α copies. Take v
opposite t in B₁ (distance D₁ = 6) in a copy j not adjacent to (t,k). A path from (t,k) to v in copy j starts with an
edge to a copy of t, changes copy at least once (only new vertices join different copies: 2 edges per change), and
its projection to B₁ joins t to v (≥ 6 edges). So the distance is ≥ 1 + 2 + 6 = 9. This uses only "a new vertex
touches copies of a single vertex of B₁ (as in the compound notation of §2.8) in fewer copies than there are", so no
wiring of the copies with the thesis's degrees and counts gives 8.

## Round 1 summary (2026-10-24, Opus + 16 Haiku cards)

Round 1 assumed the table graph was a zero-waste lift of "H(9) + 2 hop matchings" by a group of order 35 and tried to
reconstruct it: random, structured and annealed voltages on H(q) + hops (q = 5..9), H(7) + 4 hops, H(8) + 3 hops,

research/squads/LOG.md (round 1) and research/d12D8/README.md. In hindsight the lift was never there to find.
