## Why the cells did not fall

- The DGQ count 5⌊(q+5)/2⌋ is about 2.5q + 12.5 copies. Every mechanism we can prove or measure gives 2q + O(1):
  untwisted designs (Theorem 2.3, robust form), the duality family (2q + 3 robust), and the best searches (17 at q = 7).
  The extra 0.5q + 10 copies need an idea we do not have: probably hexagon voltages (a genuine cover of I(H(q))) whose
  geodesic sums are controlled for all pairs, which none of our coordinate voltages achieves (they behave like random
  labels, r1 c4, c13; d14D8.md).
- Random or annealed lifts are coupon-collector limited (about 9 walks of length 7 per target at the table size; the
  table needs nearly perfect hashing).
- Haar graphs: (10,8) is within reach of the orbit-symmetric Haar search (cost-6 states at 1,177,968; separate grind
  branches), the other three cells are 19–50% short. Not run here (brief).
