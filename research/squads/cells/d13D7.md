## How

1. The table value is three copies of the incidence graph of the split Cayley hexagon H(11) plus one matching σ from
   points of copy i to lines of copy i+1. Any bijection σ works.
2. Surgery: at a set R of pairwise opposite points, delete the three matching edges of each r ∈ R and add two hubs
   (one joined to (r,0),(r,1),(r,2), one to (σ(r),0),(σ(r),1),(σ(r),2)), hubs linked by a circulant of degree 10.
   This keeps diameter 7 when σ maps the points collinear with r onto the lines meeting σ(r) (a local duality) and
   σ(R) is pairwise opposite (Hermitian spread). Gain: +2 per point of R.
3. So the cell reduces to a large partial ovoid of the polar space Q(6,11) (pairwise non-orthogonal points; no ovoid
   exists for q = 11; the true maximum is unknown, the bound is 1331).

## What failed and why

- Haar / Cayley search: at N ≈ 531,500 the Poisson mean of uncovered elements is about 1,160 against a ceiling of about
  171 (STRUCTURE.md lists D = 7, Δ ≥ 9 above the first-moment ceiling). Not run.
- Four or more copies at Δ = 13: the copies two levels apart are too far (exp2 measured about 390 uncovered targets per
  source); a 3-cycle of copies always contains one H → H transition (H(11) is not self-dual), so the local-duality
  condition cannot be avoided.
- Bigger cliques in the fibration graph: DLS-MC found 39,089 distinct 29-cliques and no 30 (r2 c1); fixed-k tabu,
  symmetric cliques, the square-class direction (20 lines) and two-direction fibrations gave nothing (r2 c2, c3, c5).
- A relaxed surgery that allows distance-4 pairs in R ("stars" around a middle point) works on the point side (1,331
  points from an ovoid of the GQ Q(4,11) at a point), but the line side must mirror the stars, and for lines of H(11)
  the forbidden choices are incoherent (research/d13D7/relax/cohere*.c: 110-121 distinct pairs where coherence needs
  11). The line-star search (r2 c8) reached 128 lines in 121 stars, far below 320. Closed for q = 11.
- The 364 orbit set is LNS-rigid (r3 c2, c4); crossovers of 397 and 364 return to 397 (r3 c4).
- Every structured method beat random-start local search by 2-3x. LNS found a (1,62) swap no clique search could see
  (397), but then every local method stalled there; the jump to 720/756 came from changing the symmetry class searched
  (orbit unions of small abelian groups with an exact maximum-weight clique). 720 is locally rigid (no outside point
  is blocked by fewer than 45 set points).

## Ceiling of this method

At most 2·1331 extra vertices (1,065,598) even with a perfect partial ovoid (none exists for q = 11). Larger gains
need a different construction; for self-dual hexagons (q = 3^h) the same surgery with an explicit duality allows any
non-collinear R, which is how (11,7) reached 406,496 (research/d13D7/REPORT.md §7).
