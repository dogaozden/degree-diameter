## What the table value is
2,480,184 = 7·|H(11)| = 7 · 354,312: the (2k+1)-copy construction with q = 11, k = 3 (Δ = q + 1 + k). Copies
c ∈ Z_7 of the incidence graph of the split Cayley hexagon H(11), cross edges (p,c) ~ (φ_j(p), c+j), j = 1, 2, 3,
for any bijections φ_j: points → lines. Every bijection gives diameter 7 (exp2-solo and exp2-squad reports, branch
claude/exp2-squad-haar-search-614l8w, research/squads/exp2/hexd7/README.md). Among copy designs this is the best at
Δ = 15: 3·H(13) = 2,413,404, 11·H(9) = 1,461,460, and 2k+1 copies is the limit of any copy design (exp2-solo §4.3).

## The construction that beats it: hubs on 7 copies
Take φ_1 = φ_2 = φ_3 = M. A *freed flag* (p, l) (p on l, M(p) = l) loses p's 21 cross edges; add 3 line-side hubs
adjacent to the 7 copies of p and 3 point-side hubs adjacent to the 7 copies of l. Degrees stay ≤ 15 (hubs: 7 + at
most 8 hub-graph edges). **n = 2,480,184 + 6K for K freed flags.** (The exp2-squad trick on 3 copies gives +2 per
freed pair; on 7 copies the same bijection frees 3 slots at each end, so +6.)

Lemma (header of `research/d15D7/kcopy_hub.c`): in a bipartite graph, diameter ≤ 7 iff same-side distances are ≤ 6.
A point pair in copies c, c+j (j = 1..3) is joined by (a,c) → (M(a),c+j) → hexagon (≤ 5) unless a is freed; a line
pair in copies c, c−j by (l,c) → (M⁻¹(l),c−j) → hexagon (≤ 5) unless M⁻¹(l) is freed; same-copy pairs by the hexagon.
So the graph has diameter ≤ 7 iff the freed points (p,0), the freed lines (l,0) and the hubs have eccentricity ≤ 7
(the graph is invariant under the copy rotation, hubs fixed). The full symmetric BFS agrees on K = 1.

What makes a freed flag work (exp2-squad's algebraic cover): S'(p) = {u: d(u,p)=4, d(u,l)=5} and S(l) = {N:
d(N,l)=4, d(N,p)=5} both have q⁴ = 14,641 elements; every point opposite p is on exactly one line of S(l), every line
opposite l contains exactly one point of S'(p). If M maps S'(p) onto S(l), then (p,0) ⇝ (u,0) → (M(u),j) → (x,j)
and (l,0) ⇝ (M(y),0) → (y,−j) → (t,−j) reach everything the hubs miss. Weaker and enough: every line of S(l) has
M⁻¹ at distance 4 from p, every point of S'(p) has M at distance 4 from l.

Hub graph: any two line-side hubs and any two point-side hubs must be within 6. A bipartite graph on one hub of each
side per flag, degree ≤ 8, in which any two vertices of a side have a common neighbour, does it: complete bipartite
(K ≤ 8), rook grids n×m with n + m ≤ 9 (K ≤ 20), PG(2,r) incidence (K = 7, 13, 21, 31, 57).
