## 2. Polygons and buildings (ruled out, with numbers)

- **GQ level graphs** (the D = 5 analogue of the D = 7 hexagon level graphs, research/theorems/D7_HEXAGON.md): order
  (2r+1)·2(q+1)(q²+1) with Δ = q+1+r. These are the 2013 values for large Δ ((10,5) = 4000 is q = 7, r = 2;
  (12,5) = 8200 is q = 9, r = 2). At Δ = 6 the best is q = 4, r = 1: **510**, far below 684. Surgery and hubs added
  0.5–4 % at D = 7; 684/510 needs +34 %.
- **Lifts of polygon incidence graphs**: a k-fold cover of a girth-g graph has diameter ≥ g − 1 (research/lifts,
  proved there), so lifts of GQ (g = 8) or GH incidence graphs cannot have diameter 5. Lifts of PG(2,5) (g = 6)
  fail on the fibre pairs (x, g) – (x, h): no 4-cycle through x.
- **Quotients of the GH(5,5) incidence graph** (7,812 vertices, diameter 6): a same-side 4-ball is 781 of 3,906
  points (20 %). A free quotient by H needs |H| | 3906 and 7812/|H| > 684, so |H| ≤ 9; an orbit of size 9 misses the
  ball with probability ≈ 0.8⁹ (≈ 58 of 434 orbits per point; ≈ 140 of 558 for |H| = 7). Ruled out by counting.
- **Weyl-distance graphs of all finite buildings** (weyl_graphs.py from the (16,5) run, rerun with maximum degree 6,
  `research/fresh65/weyl_scan6_out.txt`): the only diameter-5 member above 500 vertices is the **A₃(2) chamber graph,
  630 vertices** (X = {s₃, s₂s₁}, four equivalent choices). Our bi-coset tool rebuilds it (apsp: RESULT VALID d=6
  D=5 n=630).
- **Route-chart lifts** (the (16,5) construction): Δ = 6 = 3 letters × F₂ needs a 3-regular controller; those are
  perfect only up to m = 12 (BEAT_16_5_SQUAD.md §2), i.e. 384 vertices. 2 letters × F₃ is a cycle controller.
