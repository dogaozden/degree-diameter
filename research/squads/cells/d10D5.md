## The construction: hubs on the DM lift

The 4,800 graph: Z6-lift of the incidence graph of W(7) (400 points, 400 lines) plus a bijection σ: points → lines whose
edge {p, σ(p)} is doubled, voltages 1 and 4; flag voltages ν ∈ {0, 3}. Z6 = Z2 × Z3: the Z3 part of a walk's voltage is
its signed number of jumps, the Z2 part the ν-parity (switching a jump between the two parallel copies toggles it).

**Freeing a point p.** Delete the 12 lift edges (p,h)–(σ(p),h+1), (p,h)–(σ(p),h+4). Each vertex of the fibres of p and
of l = σ(p) now has 2 spare degrees. Add two hubs joined to the whole fibre (p, 0..5) and two joined to (l, 0..5):
+4 vertices, hubs of degree 6.
- A hub joined to a whole fibre reaches every lift vertex within 1 + 4 = 5 (W(7) has diameter 4 and the hub sees all
  sheets). A freed vertex changes sheet in 2 steps through its hub, so it reaches every vertex at base distance ≤ 3.
- What remains: (a) from (p,0), "coset 1" (both sheets) of each of the 343 points u opposite p, in 4 steps without p's
  jump. The walks are p–L–x–σ(x)–u with x collinear with p, or p–w–σ⁻¹(w)–σ(u)–u with w through p.
  Dually for l: the lines m opposite l need l–y–M–σ⁻¹(M)–m (M concurrent with l) or l–y–σ(y)–z–σ(z) = m.
  The other coset of such a pair comes from the target's own jump, or, when the target is freed too, from the
  reverse of the target's own coverage.
  (b) Same-side hubs of two freed points must be within 4: automatic if the freed points are collinear (2 + 2),
  otherwise they need a common neighbour. Each hub has 4 spare edges for a "hub graph" (line-side hub to point-side hub).
  (c) The coset-0 condition (A)/(R) of DM_D5.md for all pairs, with the routes through freed jumps removed.

**Exact coverage.** For a point p and a line g through p, the lines meeting g away from p partition the points opposite
p (each opposite point has a unique projection on g). For a line l and a point g' on l, the points collinear with g'
off l meet each line opposite l exactly once. So (a) holds if σ⁻¹ maps those 49 lines into p^⊥ and σ maps those 49
points into l^⊥. For a FLAG (p on l, g = l, g' = p) both conditions say the same thing: σ maps the 49 points collinear
with p off l onto the 49 lines meeting l away from p. Several flags make a bipartite perfect-matching problem for σ
(hubs/flagmatch.py, hubs/flaggreedy.py). Random greedy flag sets reach k = 5 with a perfect matching.

**Pipeline.** flaggreedy.py (flags + σ by matching) → dmfast_hub.c (research/d10D7/dmfast.c with freed jumps removed
and σ swaps that keep the matching structure; anneals ν for the coset-0 condition) → dmhub.c (hubs, hub graph by a
small annealing, exact cost = pairs at distance > 5 by bit-parallel BFS from the 800 sources (v,0) and the hubs;
can anneal σ/ν further) → apsp.c. build_hub.py rebuilds the edge list from a .sol independently of dmhub.c.

## What failed, and why

- Route-chart lifts with Δ = 10 = 5 letters × GF(2) (first session): 2,880 at best, far below.
- Plain variants of the DM lift (squad r1 c7, and counting in DM_D5.md): groups of order 7 or 8 over W(7) with a
  doubled matching (coverage of a coset per pair is ~2.4% at random, no algebraic design found), Z6 lifts of the
  biregular GQ(7,9) = T2*(O) with line–line edges (581,923 bad pairs at random; structurally dead: collinear points
  have one short walk).
- Slack in the 4,820 graph (r1 c6): only 4 spare degree units; no vertex can be attached; no edge can be removed.
- Freed points on one line / freed lines through one point: transversal conflicts (k = 3 stuck at ~20 holes).
- Unstructured exact flag sets stop at k = 6..7 (Hall obstruction: point triads of W(7) have 1 or 8 centres,
  line triads 0 or 2 transversals; r1 c8). Symmetric sets: one 8-orbit of an order-8 element is perfect (r1 c5);
  orders 9, 10, 12 give no perfect orbit, order-5 orbits never pair up, the 8-orbit has no exact one-flag extension
  (r2 c1, c5). Uncrowded sets (pairwise opposite points, disjoint lines) are worse, not better: k = 6 (r3 c3).
- The coset-0 annealing stalled at 5..30 pairs for every card until the targeted route move (σ(v), σ(u) of a bad
  pair made to meet); with it and a short hot polish, coset-0 has always gone to 0.

## Limits (as of this session)

- Symmetric skeletons: PGSp(4,7) has no semiregular element of projective order 16 (the 2-part of its semisimple
  element orders is at most 8), orders 9/10/12 give no perfect orbit, and order-5 orbits never pair, so the order-8
  orbit is the largest perfect symmetric flag set found.
- Hubs are not the limit: exact hub distances allow up to 20 flags (40 + 40 hubs), and k = 10 hub graphs exist with
  hub-sourced cost 0 (r2 c4, and dmhub HGDIST).
- Coverage is the limit. Counting does not bind (per freed point the supply is about 500 against 343 needed; r3 c4),
  but the structure does: in every 10-flag attempt one opposite point of a "crowded" freed point (one collinear with
  three other freed points, which have no jumps) stays uncovered; all 424 swaps that cover it cost ≥ 5 elsewhere
  (r3 c1, tabu), and annealing (25 min, joint with the hub graph, with flag moves), beam search with visited-state
  hashing, reheating, de-crowding (r3 c2) and other 10th flags (chosen by exact-matching deficiency, 8 at best) all
  end at 1..7 holes; so did freeing the hole point itself (69:214, so a hub edge could patch it: 5 holes) and a
  25-minute slow anneal of the best 1-hole state followed by a beam repair (1 hole). 10 flags may still be possible with a better finisher (ejection chains of 2-3 swaps), but
  each step is only +4 vertices; the construction's ceiling looks like 4,840..4,850.
- The same hub construction applies unchanged to the other DM cells (11,5) 7,020, (12,5) 9,840, (14,5) 17,568
  (W(8), W(9), W(11)): +4 per freed flag, spare hub degree q+3-6. Not tried here (other jobs' cells).

### FLAGDEL: a third hub per side (+6 per flag instead of +4)

- For a freed flag (p, l = sigma(p)) also delete the 6 lift edges of the flag p-l itself. Every fibre vertex of p and
  of l then has 3 spare degrees, so 3 hubs per side fit: +6 vertices per converted flag instead of +4. Flags can be
  converted one at a time (FDSET); the others keep 2 hubs per side.
- The deleted edge is rerouted through the flag's own hubs: each of the 3 line-side hubs of the flag must be adjacent
  to a point-side hub of the same flag and vice versa (p -> z -> z' -> l is 3; y on l -> l -> z' -> z -> p is 4; lines
  through p -> l is 4; distance-3 pairs through the edge get 5).
- What breaks are the unique point-line 3-geodesics through p-l. The damage that matters ("D1"): the coset-1 walks
  v -> sigma(v) -> (3-geodesic) -> u with sigma(v) through p and u on l (and dually lines sigma(y), y on l, to lines
  through p): 2 x 49 pairs per flag, about a third without another route (a jump at step 3, or two back-jumps) at
  random. The other broken geodesics only matter for freed endpoints and vanish under exact coverage (p's hubs reach
  every point collinear with p in 4). Converting all 9 flags of the 4,836 graph at once: 1,357 bad (source, target)
  pairs, 1,100 of them D1, the rest coset-0; nothing else.
- The "star" condition sigma(l - p) = lines through p would turn every D1 pair into a pair on l (hub-covered), but
  it removes the coverage slack carried by the points of l: with star imposed, coverage annealing stalls at ~660
  holes, and exact coverage + star has a 320/400 matching for the 8-orbit. Not usable.
- Pipeline (all in r4/): mkhubF.py (H = 3 hub numbering, keeps the original hub edges, adds own-flag hub pairs for the
  converted flags) -> fdrun.sh (dmhub2_f: coverage + D1 holes, targeted D1 repair moves; FIXPTS) -> hubfix.py (rewires
  hub edges by exact BFS from the hubs) -> c0run.sh (dmfast_fd3: coset-0 with the deleted flag edges removed from
  geodesics and routes; RELAX=1 keeps coverage and D1 at every swap, RELAX=2 allows breaking them at a penalty PENW)
  -> finish_fd.sh (exact dmhub2_f cost, independent rebuild build_fd.py, apsp).
- Result: converting flag 1 (41:289) gave **4,838, apsp VALID** (hubs/checks/d10D5_n4838_CHECK.md). Path: coverage
  + D1 to 0 (fdrun.sh, ~3 min); coset-0 124 -> 2 pairs (dmfast_fd3, RELAX=1 then RELAX=2); the last 2 pairs were
  stuck for every annealer and for route surgery (no single or double sigma swap lowers the count: each new route
  breaks another unmixed pair's route). What closed it: lns2.py FLIPS=1 BESTFLIP=1, i.e. flip the nu of the flag
  (on a bad pair's geodesic) whose flip creates the fewest new unmixed-and-unrouted pairs, then repair those exactly
  with CP-SAT on sigma (coverage, D1 and all unmixed pairs' routes as constraints); 2 -> 1 -> 0 in three moves.
- The coset-0 endgame is the hard part of FLAGDEL: every nu flip unmixes ~20 pairs (a flag lies on 4q^3
  geodesics), and every route sigma(a) ~ sigma(b) created by a swap removes others. Points on freed lines and
  preimages of lines through freed points must be allowed into the CP-SAT neighbourhood (KZ) for the unconverted
  flags, or some pairs can never be routed.
