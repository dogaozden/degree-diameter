## How

The (16,5) route-chart construction (research/theorems/BEAT_16_5_SQUAD.md §2, branch claude/beat-16-5-squad-jiiwg2)
carried over to F_3. The pencil lemma holds for any q (over F_3 the 8 vectors Z(i,j) must meet the 4 hyperplanes
through <b_i, b_j>). Over F_3 the universal condition is tight: the 12 lines A_j^-1<b_i, b_j> form four 3-line
pencils of 10 points each, which would have to partition the 40 points of PG(3,3), so singular patterns are hard to
avoid. chart3.c (hill climbing) finds pencil-complete charts with 18 singular patterns of 216 (F_4 reached 8). With
that chart, mlift_chart.c (unchanged from the (16,5) run; it only reads the pattern table) reaches cost 0 on
K_{3,3} + matching over Z_18 (m = 54) for most of the 24 colour relabellings within seconds, and cost 4 over Z_22
(m = 66).
