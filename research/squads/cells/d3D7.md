## 0. What is known before this job

- The 168 graph is arc-transitive (|Aut| = 504), girth 12. It is the lift of the 2+2 cubic base
  (biadjacency ((2,1),(1,2))) over C2 × (C7 ⋊ C3) = SmallGroup(42,2), voltages 0 21 0 0 5 2 (`seeds/conder168.edges`).
  `liftx` finds exactly 168 assignments for that group and none for the other five groups of order 42.
- Lab work before this job (voltage-lifts job, Oct 23): 375 lift anneals of 20 s (Haar, all bases up to 6+6, 200
  random 7+7, "generic" groups, orders 170–200): best cost 10. Haar wave 4: 59 groups of order 85–100 × 10 s: cost 10.
- A cubic bipartite graph has diameter ≤ 7 iff same-side distances are ≤ 6. From a vertex, at most
  1 + 6 + 24 + 96 = 127 same-side vertices lie within distance 6, hence 254.

## 2. Calibration of the heuristics

- Plain annealing of unstructured cubic bipartite graphs (`sa3`, edge switches, exact all-pairs cost): cost 2 at
  n = 130 and 58 at n = 150 after 60 s. The unstructured ceiling is about 130, far below 168.
- Joint base+voltage annealing (`salift`, T0 = 10, T1 = 0.3) rediscovers a 168 graph with C7 ⋊ C3 (p = 4) in < 1 s and
  with C14 (p = 6) in ~45 s, but is stuck at cost 20 with C7 (p = 12) after 90 s.
