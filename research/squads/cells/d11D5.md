## Best result

No improvement on 7,020 this job. The most promising open direction is the DM lift over non-quadrangle bases
(research/d11D5/NOTES.md, "The open lead"). The structural cost function there is exact and fast (4 ms), the counting
ceiling of the family is 14,796, and W(8)+1 gets to within one failing pair. Suggested next steps: (1) joint annealing
of base, σ and the flag cocycle ν on one cost, the way dmfast does for W(q); (2) two-pair insertions with σ(x₁*) = l₂*,
which give the new vertices short closed jump walks by construction; (3) the same tools on W(7)+1 (4,812 at (10,5))
as a smaller test case.

## Why the other methods won't do it

- Lifts of W(q) + parallel jumps: the reachable sheets are 2(2p+1) unless the geodesics cover Z₃, and they don't
  (W(3) UNSAT, every search far from 0). 7,020 is the maximum of that family at Δ = 11.
- Chart lifts need Δ = k·q with k ≥ 3; 11 is prime, and mixed letter/unit versions stop at 2,916.
- Haar/Cayley graphs: the size law gives about 5,300 at (11,5).
