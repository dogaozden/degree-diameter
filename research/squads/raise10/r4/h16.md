# h16 (5,10)/(6,10): Sidon-type constraint generalised (differences of S in the quotient Z_k, weighted)
Goal: a better constraint than Sidon, then a raise. r2/e14 found holes are distance-11 tails, one per Z_k-fibre, and that a Sidon
condition on the Z_k-coordinates of S cuts random-S hole rates; r3/g03 confirmed it removes catastrophic S but the median moves little.
Strategy: measure, for 200 random Sidon S on Z1453 x| Z33 (or Z6397 x| Z41), the correlation of the final cost with simple quotient
statistics: the number of distinct alternating words of length <= 9 in Z_k (the quotient ball), the minimum over j of the number of
walks reaching fibre j, the "Sidon of order 2" property (sums of two differences distinct). Then search with the best statistic as a
filter (reject S whose statistic is poor before the full evaluation) in hmeta_sidon.c. Write NOTES.md; a solve is a bonus (check with
build_haar2.py + apsp). Folder r4/h16/.
