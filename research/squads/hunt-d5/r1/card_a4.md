# Card a4 (round 1)

Read ../r1/PREAMBLE.md first. Your folder: research/squads/hunt-d5/r1/a4/ (create it).

(5,5) lifts of 2+2 bases over groups of order 97..110, and of 4+4 bases over groups of order 49..56.
Base = 5-regular bipartite multigraph on 2+2 (or 4+4) vertices, connected (enumerate the multiplicity patterns; for
4+4 sample ~20 random 5-regular bipartite multigraphs with few parallel edges). `flift BASE TABLE.bin 5 5 5 SEED`.
Groups: all non-abelian groups of these orders (gapgroup.py), plus cyclic as a control. Not yours: 3+3 (card a3).
