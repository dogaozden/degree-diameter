# c3: exceptional finite subgroups of G2(11) from the integral octonions
G2(2) (order 12,096, the automorphism group of the integral octonions / Coxeter's integral Cayley numbers), its derived
group U3(3) (6,048), 2^3.L3(2) (1,344, the stabiliser of an octonion frame), PSL(2,7) (168), PSL(2,8) (504), and
PSL(2,13) (1,092) all embed in G2(q) for suitable q; reduce mod 11. Build the octonion multiplication (Fano-plane
table on imaginary units e1..e7), write generators of these groups as 7x7 matrices on the imaginary octonions over
F_11 (sign changes + permutations along the Fano lines give 2^3.L3(2); add the order-3/4 elements mixing units that
come from the integral (Coxeter) octonions to get G2(2)), verify each is an automorphism (preserves the product, hence
the form sum x_i^2), conjugate to the standard form (ovlib.conjugate_to_standard with gram = 2*identity), and run
orbclq on the groups and on their subgroups (orders ~10..300: 7:3, 2^3:7, S4, A4, 4^2:S3, 3^2:Q8, etc.).
Report the best invariant set and the group.
