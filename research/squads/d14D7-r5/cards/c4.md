# c4: non-abelian extensions of torus subgroups (normaliser elements)
The 756 set is invariant under an order-15 torus element h (see BRIEF). At q = 13 the record jumped from 500 (cyclic)
to 792 by adding non-commuting MONOMIAL elements (permutations of the three hyperbolic planes and swaps inside a plane)
to a torus subgroup. Do the same at q = 11. Use ovlib.torus(['E','H','H','L']) (blocks: elliptic plane, two hyperbolic
planes, a line) and ovlib.torus(['H','H','H','L']) (split torus), get P (block basis -> standard coordinates) from it,
and build Weyl-type elements in block coordinates, then conjugate by P: swapping the two vectors of a hyperbolic plane
(e <-> f), swapping two hyperbolic planes, conjugation (Frobenius) on the elliptic plane, -1 on the last line, and
products. Groups G = <torus subgroup H, Weyl elements> of orders ~20..400 (H cyclic of order 3, 5, 6, 10, 12, 15, 30 or
bicyclic 5x5, 3x5, 10x10 ...). Check each generator with ovlib.is_isometry. Run orbclq; report the best.
