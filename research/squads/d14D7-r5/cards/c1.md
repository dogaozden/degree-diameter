# c1: principal PGL(2,11) (binary sextics) and its subgroups
The 7-dim irreducible representation of SL(2,11) on binary sextics f(x,y) = sum a_i x^(6-i) y^i (Sym^6 of the natural
2-dim module; irreducible since 6 < 11) preserves a symmetric bilinear form (the 6th transvectant). PGL(2,11) acts
projectively (twist by det^-3 for elements of non-square determinant if needed; or use similitudes, orbclq accepts
similitudes). Build the 7x7 matrices of generators of SL(2,11) / GL(2,11) acting on sextics (substitution
(x,y) -> (ax+by, cx+dy)), find the invariant form with ovlib.invariant_forms, conjugate to the standard form
(ovlib.conjugate_to_standard), and run orbclq for PGL(2,11), PSL(2,11) and their subgroups: A5, A4, S4 (if in PGL),
dihedral groups D_10, D_12, D_20, D_24, cyclic 5, 6, 10, 12, Frobenius 11:5, 11:10, and products of these with -1.
Also try the 5-dim module Sym^4 plus a 2-dim complement, and Sym^2 + Sym^2-type decompositions (SO(3) x SO(4) inside
SO(7)): a group acting on a 3-dim and a 4-dim orthogonal piece separately, e.g. A5 acting on both (icosahedral
rotations in SO(3,11) and via SL(2,11) x SL(2,11) on the 4-dim piece).
Report the best G-invariant set.
