# c2: the Janko group J1 < G2(11) and its subgroups
J1 (order 175,560) lies in G2(11) < O(7,11). Generators (Janko's Y of order 7 and Z of order 5, already conjugated to the
standard form): research/squads/d14D7-r5/j1_gens.txt (check: `orbclq j1_gens.txt -t 1` shows 6 orbits of sizes 1596,
5852, 17556, 29260, 35112, 87780; no orbit is good, so J1 itself is too big). Maximal subgroups of J1: PSL(2,11) (660),
2^3:7:3 (168), 2 x A5 (120), 19:6 (114), 11:10 (110), D6 x D10 (60), 7:6 (42). Find these and their subgroups (orders
roughly 10..200) inside the matrix group: take random words in Y, Z, compute element orders (ovlib.mat_order), get
elements of orders 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 10, 11, 15, 19, and build subgroups as normalisers / pairs of elements whose
generated group has the right order (ovlib.group_order works up to ~10^5 elements). Run orbclq on each subgroup
(write its generators with ovlib.write_gens) and report the best invariant set. Sporadic symmetry might give an
exceptional big partial ovoid (as 2.A5 did in the elliptic section: two cosets of 2.A5 gave 240 there).
