# c4: point-side partition structure (partial stars)
For two orthogonal singular points u, v with the projective line <u,v> totally singular but not a hexagon line, the
12 points of <u,v> are pairwise at distance 4 with one common midpoint m, and every base point is orthogonal to exactly
one of them (or to all). Such X need the line side to mirror a STAR: the lines must all meet one line M = sigma(m) at
different points (pairwise distance 4) and M must meet no other line of the configuration; mirror_build.py currently
assumes X pairwise opposite, so extend it (copy into your folder) to allow X-internal distance-4 pairs whose midpoints
map to the connecting lines (stars of size up to 12 at one point/line), and re-check exact mirroring (including that no
other configuration line meets M). Then use partial stars (a subset of the 12 points, those non-collinear with R756) as
building blocks, alone or mixed with single extra points. Report the best F with your extended builder (it must
re-check everything; explain the extension in one line).
