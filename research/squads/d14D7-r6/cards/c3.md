# c3: degree-<=2 constructions
Rare types come from base points near 3 or more extra points. Build X so that NO base point is at distance 4 from three
extra points (triple intersections of the neighbourhoods N(x) empty) and pair intersections are small; e.g. greedy or
max-independent-set style selection on the candidate list with that constraint, maximising |X| (up to about 24 is
possible by counting). Then search lines L (pairwise opposite, not in S) to match: each pair type {i, j} needs
>= |N(x_i) n N(x_j)| spread lines adjacent exactly to L_i and L_j, singletons likewise. Use annealing on the lines only
(points fixed). Report the best F (mirror_build.py).
