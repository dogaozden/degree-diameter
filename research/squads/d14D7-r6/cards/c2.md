# c2: exact optimisation on a fixed line set (ILP / branch and bound)
Fix the lines L (e.g. the 11 of configs/state_k11_sa4.npz plus several extra pool lines), so spread-line types are
fixed. Then choosing the extra points is a selection problem: pick at most one candidate x per line (x opposite to the
other chosen x's), maximise (#pairs) - (sum over types of max(0, #base points of the type - #spread lines of the type)).
Restrict candidates per line to a short list (e.g. the 200 candidates whose neighbourhood best fits that line's spread
neighbourhood), then solve with an ILP (pulp/OR-tools if installed, else your own branch and bound) or a good heuristic.
Iterate: re-pick lines given the points. Report the best F.
