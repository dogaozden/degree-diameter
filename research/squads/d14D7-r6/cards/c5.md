# c5: more spread-like lines (second spread)
f(R756) must be pairwise opposite lines; we use the Hermitian spread S (lines in x0 + x4 = 0). Other Hermitian spreads
S(n) are the hexagon lines inside n^perp for nonsingular n with elliptic n^perp. Lines of S(n) are mostly not opposite
to lines of S, but a mixed set f(R) from S and S(n) can be pairwise opposite if chosen carefully, which gives more line
types to match the base points. Copy mirror_build.py and generalise f to take lines from S + S(n) (still pairwise
opposite, re-checked), and adapt the search. Report the best F.
