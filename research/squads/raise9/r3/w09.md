# w09: WILDCARD (structure): reuse a solved D = 9 graph to build a bigger one

Every value here is a Haar graph found by search. Look for a construction that takes a SOLVED Haar graph H(G,S) of diameter 9
(files research/frontier/sols/d<Δ>D9_*.txt; small ones: (6,9) d6D9_sl_17_13_orb3f0_s2.txt, (7,9) d7D9_psl_29_19_orb3_s1.txt,
(5,9) d5D9_meta_346_43_29_s613_n29756.txt) and produces a larger graph of the same degree and diameter: e.g. a 2-cover or
Z_m-cover H(G x Z_m, S') with lifted voltages chosen so the diameter stays 9 (that is a Haar graph of G x Z_m with S' =
{(s, c_s)}: try annealing only the c_s in Z_m, starting from the solved S); or a cover of G by a non-split extension. The
size law says the cover will usually fail at D = 9, but the covering structure may beat random S at the same N. Test on (5,9)
or (6,9) where evaluations are cheap; report the best cost per m. Folder r3/w09/.
