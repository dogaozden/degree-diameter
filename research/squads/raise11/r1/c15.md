# c15: wildcard 2: another group family at N ~ 5e6-2.3e8 with fast evaluation
Find a group family not yet used at (7..10,11) that has no or few involutions, a small centre, and an automorphism
of order 3 with few fixed points, and test it with tools that exist: haar_gen3s supports meta, sl/psl/pgl over GF(q)
(q a prime power), agl1, products ("prod G1 G2") and x Z_n (HEADER). Ruled out: Frobenius products and SL x Frobenius
beyond Δ = 5, 2-dim kernels, F_q x| C_k with q = p^3, PSL(2,p^e) with Frobenius automorphism, Sz(8), PGL Cayley graphs,
agl1 products. Other cards: c01-c02 Frobenius farms at (6,11); c03-c08 SL/PSL orbit farms; c09-c14 measurements and
ideas. Candidates to consider: SL(2,q) for q = p^2 (q = 49, 121, 169...) times Z_n; SL(2,p) x SL(2,p') products
(odd D: only the central +-I's); SU(3,q)-like groups are not supported (skip). Measure random-S statistics
(haar_gen3s with a short time box gives the cost reached) against SL(2,p) x Z_n at the same N for (7,11) (N ~ 5.5-5.6e6).
Report: family, N, lowest cost, comparison.
