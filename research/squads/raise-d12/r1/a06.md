# a06: (4,12) lifts of the incidence graph of H(3) (split Cayley hexagon of order 3); beat 70,176
Idea: a regular voltage lift of a girth-12, diameter-6 base (H(3): 364 points, 364 lines, 728 vertices, 4-regular) over a
voltage group of order m: 728 m vertices; m >= 97 beats 70,176 (97: 70,616; 100: 72,800; 105: 76,440). The base has
no short cycles, so short walks in the lift never collide (Haar graphs of small groups lose words there).
Base: `$B/hexgen2 3` -> hex/hex_q3.bin (format in research/d10D7/hexgen2.c). Verify girth 12, diameter 6.
Voltages: spanning tree edges get e, the 729 cotree edges get group elements; lift (u,g) ~ (v, g a). Groups (own code):
Z_m, Z_a x Z_b, metacyclic Z_a x|_r Z_b, Frobenius Z_p x| Z_k (e.g. Z97, Z101 x| Z... , Z_53 x| Z_2 ...), orders 90-110.
Cost = #(base vertex u, lift vertex x) with dist((u,e), x) > 12: 728 BFS of ~70k vertices per evaluation (bitset BFS
in C, ~0.1-0.5 s); to keep it fast, evaluate cost on a random sample of 64 base vertices during annealing and the full
728 only for candidates at sampled cost 0. Annealing moves: change one voltage, prefer edges near uncovered pairs.
Map cost vs m (85..105) at a fixed budget first. Scorer: edge list + `$B/apsp EDGES 4 12`. Memory cap 150 MB.
