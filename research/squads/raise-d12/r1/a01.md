# a01: (3,12) lifts of the Tutte 12-cage (incidence graph of the generalized hexagon H(2)); beat 2,368
Idea: a regular voltage lift of a base graph with girth 12 and diameter 6 (a Moore-type base: no short cycles, so short
walks never collide) over a voltage group of order m has 126 m vertices and girth >= 12. m >= 19 beats 2,368
(19: 2,394; 20: 2,520; 21: 2,646; 24: 3,024). Untried in this lab (own-small used 4+4 bases, beyond-d11b cubic multigraphs).
Base: `$B/hexgen2 2` writes hex/hex_q2.bin (63 points, 63 lines, 3 points per line); incidence graph = 126 vertices, cubic,
189 edges. Verify: bipartite, girth 12, diameter 6 (BFS). Voltages: pick a spanning tree, tree edges get e, the 64
cotree edges get group elements; lift vertex (u,g), base edge u-v with voltage a gives (u,g) ~ (v, g a) (u on the
point side). Groups to try (write your own multiplication): Z_m, dihedral D_m (order 2m), Z_a x| Z_b metacyclic and
Z_a x Z_b, orders 19-30. Cost = number of pairs (base vertex u, lift vertex x) with dist((u,e), x) > 12: left
multiplication by the group is a fibre-preserving automorphism, so 126 BFS (one per base vertex) give the exact
diameter. Search: simulated annealing over the 64 voltages (move: change one voltage), write it in C (bitset BFS).
Measure first how cost depends on m at a fixed budget (m = 16..24), then push the largest m that reaches 0.
Scorer: build the edge list (n = 126 m), run `$B/apsp EDGES 3 12` (it must say diameter <= 12). Folder r1/a01/.
