# g06: bipartite CAYLEY graphs of an index-2 overgroup (new family) at (5,11), (6,11), (5,12), (6,12)
Idea: instead of a Haar graph on G, take Gamma containing G0 with index 2 and T inside Gamma \ G0 with T = T^-1, |T| = Δ.
Cay(Gamma, T) is bipartite (parts G0, Gamma \ G0), has |Gamma| = 2|G0| vertices (same size as a Haar graph on G0) and is
VERTEX-TRANSITIVE: one BFS from the identity gives the diameter (Haar graphs at odd D need two). Examples:
Gamma = SL^{+-}(2,p) = {det = +-1} over G0 = SL(2,p) (T = Δ matrices of det -1, closed under inverse; an element t with
t^2 = I is allowed once); Gamma = PGL(2,p) over PSL(2,p) (T in the non-square-determinant coset); times Z_n with the
cyclic part arbitrary (keep T = T^-1). Write the group and a bitset BFS in C yourself.
Questions: (1) at equal size, are the hole counts of random T lower or higher than random S for the Haar graph on G0
(use haar_sym RANDORB=10 ORBIT="1 Δ-1 ..." or plain haar_gen3 start costs as the Haar baseline)? (2) anneal T at
(5,11) N_Gamma ~ 300-330k and (5,12) ~ 1.05-1.15M; beat 297,648 / 1,041,768. A clear "worse by X" is a useful result.
