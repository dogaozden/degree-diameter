(Δ = q+1, diameter 3) or of GQ W(q) (diameter 4) or the Heawood graph; voltages on the edges (one per edge, spanning

fibre over 2 base vertices if the lift is vertex-transitive on fibres, else from every fibre's first vertex - the lift
is Z_m-invariant, so BFS from one vertex per base vertex suffices). Read research/lifts/RESULTS.md first.
