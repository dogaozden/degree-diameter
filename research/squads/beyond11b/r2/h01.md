# h01: (4,11) Frobenius Z_p x| Z_k, small odd k (beat 28,918)
Build on g02 (r1/g02/: run1g.sh, logs). Groups `meta p k r`, p prime, k odd in 9..31, k | p-1, r of order exactly k mod p
(then the group is Frobenius: odd order, trivial centre). N = p k in 14,460-15,600 (2N > 28,918). g02's near misses
(cost 2-4 in one 95 s run): N = 14,531, 14,607, 14,735, 15,025 - start with those (several seeds each, K = 4 and 8), then
the other groups in the range, smallest N first. Plain random S: `THREADS=1 timeout 105 nice -n 10 $B/haar_gen3 4 11 100
<seed> <K> OUT meta p k r 1`. Different r of the same order give isomorphic groups (r -> r^j); one r per (p,k) suffices.
Scorer: build_haar2.py on OUT. Folder: research/squads/beyond11b/r2/h01/.
