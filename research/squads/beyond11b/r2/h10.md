# h10: orbit symmetry that suits Frobenius groups: alpha = translation scaling (haar_sym AMU)
Round-1 g16 found that ORBIT with conjugation alpha hurt on SL(2,5) x Frobenius. Frobenius groups Z_p x| Z_k have another
automorphism: (a, j) -> (u a, j) with u in Z_p^* (it commutes with the action); with u of order 3 its fixed points are only
the k elements (0, j). haar_sym has it: AMU=<u> ("meta scaling (a,j) -> (u a, j)"; read haar_sym.c lines ~780-870 for the
exact use with ORBIT). Test at (4,11) (N 14,460-15,600, beat 28,918) and (5,11) (N 150,601-158,000, beat 301,200):
ORBIT="3 1 1" (Δ = 4) / "3 1 2" (Δ = 5) with AMU = u of order 3 mod p (needs 3 | p-1), against plain haar_gen3 on the
same group: RANDORB=10 start costs, then searches. `ORBIT="3 1 1" AMU=u THREADS=1 timeout 105 nice -n 10 $B/haar_sym 4 11
100 <seed> 4 OUT meta p k r 1`. Scorer: build_haar2.py. Folder: research/squads/beyond11b/r2/h10/.
