Δ = 3 lifts beat Haar graphs. Test Δ = 5: Z_n lifts of 5-regular bipartite bases (K5,5; incidence graphs of small designs,
e.g. the 2-(11,5,2) biplane (22 vertices); random 5-regular bipartite graphs, 30-80 vertices, girth >= 6). Targets:
(5,14) beat 11,757,096 (n * b ~ 1.18e7), (5,13) beat 3,514,394. Use `DEG=5 $B/bin/liftd ...` (base: "nverts" then "u v" edges, sides [0,b/2), [b/2,b)). A lift eval is
b BFS's of n*b vertices: keep b small. Check: apsp on 3.5M+ vertices is too slow; use the lift's own symmetry: write a
checker that BFSes from one vertex per base vertex (the Z_n rotation is an automorphism), in your folder, and validate it
against apsp on a small lift first. Score: largest VALID graph (cell in "cell").
