# c11 (5,14): orbit forms with an automorphism of order 2 or 4 (haar_big0) against random S
Goal: a VALID (5,14) Haar graph with N > 6,397,583 in SL(2,p) x Z_n / PSL(2,p) x Z_n using S closed under an
automorphism alpha of order 2 or 4: ORBIT="2 2 1" (S = {e, x, ax, y, ay}: two free elements, holes come in pairs) and
ORBIT="4 1 1" (S = {e} + one 4-orbit). Order 3 ("3 1 2") stalled at cost 18 in beyond-d13; order 2 won at (6,8).
haar_big0 picks alpha itself (it needs k | p-1 or p+1 and an element of order k mod n, or n = 1). Steps:
1. For 4-6 groups with N ~ 6.5M (`python3 research/frontier/beyond14/tools/pick.py 5 14 5 9 2 2000` and the same with k = 4), RANDORB=200
   statistics: `RANDORB=200 ORBIT="2 2 1" timeout 105 nice -n 10 env THREADS=1 research/frontier/beyond14/bin/haar_big0 5 14 60 1 1 OUT sl p n`
   (median and minimum cost); compare with plain random S (haar_gen3 or ORBIT="1 4 1" if haar_big0 accepts k = 1).
2. Search the best form: `ORBIT="2 2 1" timeout 105 ... haar_big0 5 14 95 SEED 16 OUT sl p n`, chain with START.
Check: `timeout 105 python3 research/frontier/build_haar2.py SOL`. Folder: research/squads/beyond14/r1/c11/.
