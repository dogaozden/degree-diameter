# c03 (3,14): non-cyclic voltage groups on small cubic bases
Goal: a VALID cubic lift with more than 5,706 vertices using voltage group Z_a x Z_b, a dihedral group D_n, or a
metacyclic group Z_m x|_r Z_k (non-abelian voltages keep more walk classes distinct than cyclic ones; see the header).
Write your own annealer: copy research/frontier/beyond14/tools/lift.c into your folder and replace the Z_n arithmetic by a group given as a
multiplication on indices (precompute a full multiplication table: the voltage group has only ~200-500 elements).
Lift: vertices (v,g); edge e = (u,w) with voltage x_e gives (u,g) ~ (w, g x_e) (right multiplication; then left
multiplication by the group is an automorphism, so BFS from (u,e) for each base vertex u gives the exact diameter).
Cost = number of (source u, target) pairs at distance > 14, as in lift.c. Bases: Pappus (18, research/frontier/beyond14/bases/pappus_j13.txt),
GP(10,3) (20, research/frontier/beyond14/bases/gp10_3.txt), Heawood (14, research/frontier/beyond14/bases/heawood.txt), K3,3 (6). Group orders so that b |Gamma| > 5,706
(e.g. Pappus x Gamma of order 318-330, Heawood x 408-430, K3,3 x 952-1000). Measure cost after a 95-s anneal for
several groups of the same order first; then chain the best (several seeds; T0 T1 L cooling over the run).
Already tried: cyclic lifts (all bases in the header); metacyclic voltages on GP(10,3) at D = 13 (cost 2 at 3,300, weak
annealer). Check: edge list + `research/frontier/beyond14/bin/apsp`. Folder: research/squads/beyond14/r1/c03/.
