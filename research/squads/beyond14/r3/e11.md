# e11 (5,14): lifts of small 5-regular bases over large-k Frobenius groups
Goal: a VALID 5-regular bipartite lift with more than 13,105,798 vertices. Copy research/squads/beyond14/r2/d08/lift4.c into your folder and
make the degree a parameter (5 perfect matchings between whites 0..p-1 and blacks p..2p-1; check its fixed array
sizes). Use p = 2 (|G| >= 3,276,450) with Frobenius groups Z_m x| Z_k, m prime, k | m-1, k in 30-100. One evaluation at
13M vertices costs ~1 s, so calibrate first at ~12.5M (does it solve?), then above the record.
Check: report cost-0 files with the base and voltages; the director builds and checks them.
Folder: research/squads/beyond14/r3/e11/.
