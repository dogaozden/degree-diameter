# r4 c3 (q = 11): groups from the decomposition A ⊥ V4
V = {x4 = -x0} = A ⊥ V4 with A = (x0,x3) (anisotropic: Q_A = -(x0^2+x3^2)) and V4 = (x1,x2,x5,x6) (Q4 = x1x5+x2x6 =
det of the 2x2 matrix [[x1,x2],[-x6,x5]]). Isometries: rotations of A (a -> zeta a, zeta of norm 1 in GF(121) = GF(11)[i],
order 12) times maps of V4 of the form M -> X M Y^-1 with det X = det Y (X, Y in GL(2,11)), which preserve det. Build
groups H = <rotation^a> x K with K a small subgroup (cyclic of order 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 12, Klein, dihedral) of the
(X,Y) action, compute the orbits of H on the points of E (normalize projective vectors; use E11.map to index points),
and run orbitclique on each (cycle-permutation trick). Score: best VALID N.
