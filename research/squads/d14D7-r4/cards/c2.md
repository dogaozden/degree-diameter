# r4 c2 (q = 11): large-neighbourhood search around the 240 sets
Goal: beat N = 240. Start from sols/flags_q11_orbit4_240.txt and sols/flags_q11_orbit8_240.txt. Repeatedly remove a
region of the solution (e.g. all points orthogonal to a random point of E, or a random 15-25 % of the points), then
refill it as well as possible with points of E compatible with the kept part: exactly (small max-independent-set by
branch and bound on the free points) when the free region is small, or with a strong local search; accept the result
when not smaller. The orthogonality test is B(x,y) = x0y4+x4y0+x1y5+x5y1+x2y6+x6y2-2x3y3 = 0 mod 11 on coordinates from
E11.map. Score: best VALID N.
