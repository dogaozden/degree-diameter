# c12: self-dual S for odd D (code idea)
Idea: for odd D the cost is holes of H(G,S) plus holes of H(G,S^-1). If S^-1 = gamma(S) for an automorphism gamma, the
two counts are equal (H(G,S^-1) is then isomorphic to H(G,S)), so only one BFS is needed and success needs one side,
not two. For G = SL(2,p) x Z_n: tau(M,z) = (M^T, z) is an anti-automorphism, and S closed under tau gives
S^-1 = gamma(S) with gamma(M,z) = (M^-T, -z). alpha = conjugation by g commutes with tau when g is orthogonal (g^T = g^-1);
an orthogonal g of order 3 exists when 3 | p-1 and -3 is a square, or similar (work it out, or search all g of order 3
for one with g^T = g^-1 up to sign).
Task: copy research/frontier/beyond11/haar_big0.c to your folder as haar_sd.c and add a mode SD=1: S = {e} + orbits of
the group <alpha, tau> (an orbit is O = alpha-orbit of x together with the alpha-orbit of x^T), cost = 2 x (one-sided
cost). Verify on (11,9) and (13,9) (pick.py) that the full two-sided cost of your S (run haar_big0 with EVAL=<sol>, D odd)
equals 2 x your one-sided cost, and compare solve rates with plain haar_big0 at the same groups (60 s runs, 2 seeds).
Any solution must pass haarcheck_gen. Build: gcc -O3 -march=native -fopenmp -o haar_sd haar_sd.c -lm. Memory cap 0.3 GB.
Folder: research/squads/beyond11/r1/c12/. Score = largest 2N solved at (11,9) or (13,9) with SD=1.
