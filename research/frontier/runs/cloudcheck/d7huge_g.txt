## How the brief's ranges were run
Each brief range was run as 8 consecutive chunks, one chunk at a time, 4 threads each. Chunk i of a range covers LO + floor(N*i/8) to LO + floor(N*(i+1)/8), N being the range length (658135), so the chunks tile the brief's range exactly; the tiling was checked from the printed sources= fields. A range counts as covered only when all 8 of its chunks print RESULT VALID. This follows from the checker's per-source work (BFS from each source of the chunk, with the global simple/degree/bipartite/connected checks) being additive over disjoint source sets.
Reason for chunks: the worker session was restarted during the single-range runs (one range takes about 4.3 h at the speed seen earlier), and each restart killed the running checker without a result. Chunks of about 35 min let finished chunks survive a restart; a driver script skips any chunk whose output already has a RESULT line.

Runs that did not produce a result and are not counted:
- range 10 as one run, 4 threads, killed by a restart at 606208 of 658135 sources (first attempt);
- range 10 as one run, 4 threads, same arguments, killed by a restart at 278528 of 658135 sources (second attempt);
- range 11 chunk 4 (6910417..6992684), killed by a restart at 32768 of 82267 sources; it was rerun from the start and its rerun is the result below;
- a duplicate start of the range-10 run (two copies writing the same files) was noticed and both copies were killed before any output; the run was restarted once cleanly.
