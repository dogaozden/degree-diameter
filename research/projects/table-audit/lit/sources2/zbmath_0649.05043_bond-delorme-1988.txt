Bond, J. ; Delorme, C. New large bipartite graphs with given degree and diameter. (English) Zbl 0649.05043 Ars Comb. 25C, 123-132 (1988) . The double cover construction of N. Biggs [Algebraic graph theory (1974; Zbl 0284.05101 )] is used to produce large bipartite graphs of given maximal degree \(\Delta\) and diameter D. E.g. for \(\Delta =3\) and \(D=5\) a graph with 56 vertices is given. A table is presented of best known orders if \(3\leq \Delta \leq 16\) and \(3\leq D\leq Wikipedia Wolfram MathWorld 10\) with the corresponding references.
