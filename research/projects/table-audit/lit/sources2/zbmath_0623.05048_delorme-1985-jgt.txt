Delorme, C. Large bipartite graphs with given degree and diameter. (English) Zbl 0623.05048 J. Graph Theory 9, No. 3, 325-334 (1985) . We give constructions of bipartite graphs with maximum degree Wikipedia Wolfram MathWorld \(\Delta\), diameter D on B vertices, such that for every \(D\geq 2\) the lim inf\({}_{\Delta \to \infty}B\cdot \Delta^{1-D}=b_ D>0\). We also improve similar results on ordinary graphs, for example, we prove that \(\lim_{\Delta \to \infty}N\cdot \Delta^{-D}=1\) if D is 3 or 5. This is a partial answer to a problem of Bollobás.
